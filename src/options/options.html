Keyboard shortcuts

  • Cmd/Ctrl + U opens ATO with search focused.
  • ↓ / ↑ or j / k navigate outside text fields; Enter activates a tab.
  • / or Cmd/Ctrl + F focuses search. Escape returns to search, clears a query, or exits Select mode.
  • Cmd/Ctrl + click selects; Shift + click adds a visible range. Delete reviews a selection or closes the keyboard-targeted tab.
  • Cmd/Ctrl + Z undoes the latest close outside text fields.
