# ATO 3.1.0

Four new themes, a settings page that explains itself better, and a keyboard that does what I expected it to do from the search field. Nothing about permissions or privacy changed, and the settings you already saved stay as they are.

## New themes

There are now thirteen themes, plus System.

- **Daylight** is a neutral light theme where every signal is a clear mark: the current tab, copies, audio, asleep tabs, and keep or close in review. It has a little motion, which reduced motion turns off.
- **Onyx** is true black, for OLED screens.
- **Poster** is Swiss brutalist: black type, signal red, hard shadows, and the row under the cursor inverted.
- **Riso** is two fluorescent inks printed a touch off register, with halftone and paper grain. Closed rows stamp out in pink.
- **System is the default for new installs.** It follows your OS: Daylight by day (it was Paper) and Graphite by night. If you already picked a theme, it stays.

## Settings page

- **"Same page when" shows a worked example:** two URLs, the part the rule ignores struck through, and a verdict saying whether they count as the same page. It follows the rule you choose.
- The version is shown beside the name.
- A wide choice wraps under its text instead of squeezing it.
- The 3D logo is redrawn as one flat SVG. Each edge is drawn once, the corners are cut and shaded, nearer lines are heavier, and edges fade from solid to dashed as they turn away. The glitch at the outline as it turned is gone.

## Keyboard

- From an empty search field, the arrows step into the list, so ⌫ and Delete close the row under the cursor. Before, they went to the search box.
- ⌘⌫ closes from the search field: the tab under the cursor, or every result when there's a query.
- ↑ from the top of the list goes back to search instead of wrapping to the bottom.
- ← on a folded duplicate set leaves review, back onto the duplicates bar.
- Keys are drawn as keycaps, and the footer always shows the key that works right now.

## Fixes

- Light themes show clearly which view is selected, and the search ring is softer.
- "All windows" no longer wraps onto a second line.
- Big counts get separators (1,102 duplicates, Close 1,102), and the view tabs shorten past 1,000 (6k, 12.3k).
- In Cyber, "ATO" no longer looks letter-spaced.
- A boxed item under the cursor gets an even ring, without the heavy left bar.
- A stored theme value such as `constructor` or `toString` no longer breaks settings.

## Performance

- Now playing's equaliser bars scale instead of changing height, so they no longer relayout the popup. At 6,000 tabs that halved the frame time.

## Installing

ATO needs Chrome 157 or newer. The Chrome Web Store copy updates on its own. To build it yourself: `npm install && npm run build`, then load `dist/` as an unpacked extension.
