# ATO 3.3.0

A new theme, Missing Link, and a round of polish across the others.

## Missing Link

A pixel adventure: an overworld of grass and trees by day, a torch-lit stone dungeon by night, following your system's light and dark.

- Boxes are item slots with notched pixel corners; small-square tiles fill the duplicates bar and the highlighted row.
- The duplicates bar has a chest that opens when you reach it, by mouse or keyboard. Clearing every duplicate shows it open, full of gold.
- Opening or leaving Review goes through a door: the screen closes into a pixel dot, holds black for a beat, and opens on the next room.
- The logo is an 8-bit relic that turns in frames. The settings page opens like a game's title screen, its groups are game text boxes, and picking the theme there opens it through the same door.
- Labels are in VT323. Every sprite is drawn for ATO. Reduced motion turns the motion off.

It is listed first in the theme picker, marked new.

## Every theme

- The keyboard cursor on the duplicates bar shows in every theme; in Cyber and Riso it was invisible.
- The shortcuts panel reads as two clean columns, keys against what they do. It closes with a click outside it, and the arrow and Page keys scroll it.
- The logo turns while the pointer is over it, and can be dragged and flung, as on the settings page.
- Settings is a real gear (sliders in Cyber), not a sun.

## Poster and Cyber

- Poster is square everywhere, with even margins and spacing; its highlighted rows stay readable in every state (Now playing, Review sets, the × on hover).
- Cyber's large grid lines are dimmer.

## Installing

ATO needs Chrome 157 or newer. The Chrome Web Store copy updates on its own. To build it yourself: `npm install && npm run build`, then load `dist/` as an unpacked extension.
